v3_payne_0_0.ffs is the default turbo unlock driver. there is no undervolt.

first number is the core, second one is the cache undervolt amount. 

for an example if you injected v3_payne_50_20 your core is undervolted by 50mv and cache is undervolted by 20mv.

%99 of the chips are stable at -50. some chips are stable at -80. very few are stable beyond 80.

if you are hitting the power limit, your cpu will boost higher with lower voltages since it can fill the extra power with more frequency

if you are not hitting the power limit but boosting till max multiplier, undervolting will reduce your temp.

please be careful about the vrm tempratures.
